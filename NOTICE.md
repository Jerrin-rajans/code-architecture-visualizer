## Icons

Cloud provider and technology icons are supplied at runtime by
[`diagrams`](https://github.com/mingrammer/diagrams) (MIT), which bundles the
official AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Kubernetes and Elastic icon sets. Those
icons remain the property of their respective owners and are subject to each
vendor's own trademark and brand guidelines. ArchLens does not redistribute
them; they are installed as part of that package.

The tiles in [`archlens/render/assets/`](archlens/render/assets/) are original
to this project, generated by
[`tools/generate_icons.py`](tools/generate_icons.py). They are **generic role
glyphs in approximate brand colours** — a card for payments, an envelope for
email, a cylinder for a datastore — and are deliberately *not* reproductions
of any company's logo.

That distinction is the point: `diagrams` ships no icon for Stripe, so an early
version of this project fell back to another vendor's logo. Showing one
company's mark for another is worse than showing no mark at all, and drawing
the real logo would be a trademark problem. A neutral glyph in a recognisable
colour is the honest option.
